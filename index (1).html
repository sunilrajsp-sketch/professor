About Dr Lakshmidevi Rajendran

Bridging Fundamental Biochemical Kinetics with Scalable Environmental Technologies

Dr Lakshmidevi Rajendran is an Assistant Professor at Vel Tech University and an accomplished researcher with extensive academic roots at Anna University, specialising in bioprocess engineering, renewable bioenergy, and industrial wastewater valorisation.

Her research addresses a critical dual challenge: mitigating industrial pollution while generating clean energy and commercially valuable biochemicals. She has extensively investigated the enzymatic saccharification and fermentation of complex industrial wastes—including paper and pulp effluent, dairy wastewater, landfill leachate, and agro-residues—to produce clean biohydrogen and bioelectricity.

In parallel, Dr Rajendran has contributed significantly to microalgal and yeast biorefinery systems, developing mixotrophic cultivation processes in airlift photobioreactors to synthesise single-cell oils (SCO), lutein, and carotenoids. Her findings have been published in leading international peer-reviewed journals, including the International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, Biochemical Engineering Journal, Environmental Technology & Innovation, and Journal of Applied Phycology.

Research Architecture

Core Research Pillars

Our research operates at the intersection of chemical engineering, environmental biotechnology, and renewable energy, structured around four core pillars:

Clean Biohydrogen Production

Developing enzymatic and microbial fermentation pathways to convert recalcitrant lignocellulosic biomass (such as cotton stalks) and industrial effluents (paper, pulp, and dairy wastewaters) into biohydrogen using immobilised cell systems, kinetic modelling, and mass-transfer optimisation.

Bioenergy Conversion & Kinetic Modelling

Algal-Assisted Microbial Fuel Cells (MFCs)

Designing carbon-neutral bioelectrochemical systems that simultaneously treat high-strength wastewaters—such as municipal solid waste landfill leachate and dairy effluent—and generate bioelectricity by pairing immobilised bioanodes with photosynthetic microalgae.

Bioelectrochemical Systems & Effluent Treatment

Microalgal & Yeast Biorefineries

Valorising agro-industrial byproducts like crude glycerol and molasses as low-cost substrates using oleaginous yeasts and microalgal strains (Scenedesmus sp. and Synechocystis sp.) in airlift photobioreactors to synthesise lutein, carotenoids, and single-cell oils (SCO).

Bioreactor Cultivation & High-Value Metabolites

Green Extraction & Bioprocess Analytics

Developing rapid analytical protocols for microalgal biomass estimation and employing green process intensification techniques—such as ultrasound-assisted extraction—to recover natural vegetable tannins for sustainable leather processing.

Circular Economy & Ultrasonic Intensification
